Concrete result: 13 distinct adapters (built over a few months) cover 7,960 of the 10,000 most-downloaded HF embedding models, 6,804 of which pass end-to-end on-device tests; code in `torch-spyre/hf-adapters` and `torch-spyre/torch-spyre` (both public on GitHub).
