The new runtime exposes a 19-function C ABI plus JSON-RPC transport for embedding via C#/Go/Java/Python/Rust/TypeScript SDKs. Direct hit on "AI agents in practice" — a real, numbers-heavy account of agents doing a rewrite of a scale that "would have taken a team a year or two before agents."
