## 2026-W34

- **2026-08-21** — [A Tale of Two Flink Autoscalers](https://netflixtechblog.com/a-tale-of-two-flink-autoscalers-e9f6a1b1492b) — Verified via Jina (direct WebFetch 403'd): Netflix runs two Flink autoscalers in production — an original in-house one built ~2019 around Mantis/Atlas metrics, and the Apache Flink community's, which handles workload shapes the homegrown system can't — and is steadily converging on the open-source one.
