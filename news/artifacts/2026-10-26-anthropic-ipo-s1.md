Anthropic, PBC filed a Form S-1 registration statement with the U.S. SEC on October 26, 2026, for a proposed IPO of its Class A common stock, planning to list on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker "ANT"; share count and price range are not yet determined, and Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are named as prospectus contacts.
