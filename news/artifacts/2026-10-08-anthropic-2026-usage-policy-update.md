Anthropic publishes a 2026 Usage Policy update (effective Nov 12) adding rules on deceptive campaigns/fake accounts, narrowing elections rules, tightening weapons/surveillance/high-risk requirements, and adding controls for Claude connected to hardware taking autonomous physical actions.

**Деталі:**
