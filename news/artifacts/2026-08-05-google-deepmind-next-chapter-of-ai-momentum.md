Google and Alphabet CEO Sundar Pichai announces leadership changes to Google DeepMind
teams: Demis Hassabis becomes Chair of Google DeepMind and Chief Scientist of Alphabet
(while continuing to lead Isomorphic Labs); Koray Kavukcuoglu, previously DeepMind's CTO
and Alphabet's chief AI architect, steps up as SVP of Google DeepMind reporting to
Pichai, overseeing Gemini model development, Frontier AI research, and the Gemini app
and developer teams. Separately, Google Senior Fellow Jeff Dean (27-year tenure) and
Senior Fellow Sanjay Ghemawat are departing to launch an independent public benefit
corporation, Discovery Loop, aimed at automating ML/science/engineering discovery;
Google will be a founding investor and Cloud partner.
