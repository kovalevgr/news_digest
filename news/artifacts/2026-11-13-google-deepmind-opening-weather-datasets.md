Google DeepMind open-sources two datasets behind its AI weather models: SatWeave, a global satellite-imagery mosaic updated hourly, and PARDIG, an AI-generated global precipitation dataset, both published to Google Cloud Storage for researchers building weather/hydrology/climate applications.

## card
